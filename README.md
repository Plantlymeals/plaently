# Pläntly Protein Fast Food

Build a premium full‑stack DTC website for a Scandinavian plant‑based protein meal brand called PLÄNTLY. The site should compete visually with Huel and Jimmy Joy but feel more modern, lifestyle‑driven, and clean. The product is plant‑based protein meals in cups (20–25g protein per serving) that are prepared by adding hot water and ready in minutes. The website must include a modern marketing site plus an admin dashboard CMS to edit all content.

Brand design system:
Font: Poppins (use across all headings and body text).
Primary gradient background: top #64ab3d (darker green) fading to bottom #d6e9c3 (light green).
Text colors: black, white, and green #64ab3d.
Visual style: Scandinavian, minimal, premium, lots of whitespace, soft shadows, modern product marketing similar to Apple‑style landing pages. Include smooth scrolling and subtle animations.

Main navigation:
Home, Products, How It Works, Nutrition, Lifestyle, About, Blog, FAQ, Contact.
Include sticky header and primary CTA button “Shop Now”.

Homepage structure:
Hero section:
Headline: “Real food. Real protein.”
Subheadline: “Plant‑based meals with 20–25g protein per serving. Ready in minutes.”
Buttons: Shop Meals and Learn How It Works.
Hero visual: minimalist podium with product cups on gradient background.

Trust section:
Copy: “Trusted by busy professionals, athletes and modern workplaces.” Include rating placeholder and simple icons for offices, athletes, entrepreneurs, students, professionals.

Product overview section:
Grid showing 4 example meals:
Plant‑Based Fusilli Bolognese
Plant‑Based Thai Curry
Plant‑Based Creamy Mushroom
Plant‑Based Mediterranean Pasta
Each card displays protein amount, calories, and prep time.

Why PLÄNTLY section:
Icon grid listing: High protein, Plant‑based, Low sugar, Ready in minutes, Sustainable ingredients, Balanced nutrition.

How it works:
3 steps: Choose meals → Add hot water → Enjoy real plant protein.

Lifestyle section:
Headline: “Built for modern life.” Show use cases: office lunches, post‑workout recovery, travel days, quick healthy dinners.

Bundle section:
Starter Pack (12 meals), Athlete Pack (24 meals), Office Pack (60 meals), Big Office Pack (120 meals). Include badges such as “Most Popular” and “Best Value”.

Testimonials section with placeholder quotes.

Nutrition section:
Explain plant‑based protein benefits and show charts for protein, carbs, fat and calories.

Final CTA section:
Headline: “Eat smarter. Live better.” Button: Shop PLÄNTLY.

Products page:
Product grid with product detail pages including ingredients, nutrition table, allergens, preparation instructions, protein amount, and subscription purchase option.

Nutrition page:
Explain plant‑based protein science, macronutrients, and sustainability benefits.

Lifestyle page:
Content showing PLÄNTLY for athletes, offices, entrepreneurs.

About page:
Story about why PLÄNTLY exists: making healthy plant‑based protein meals simple, delicious, and sustainable for modern life.

Blog:
CMS‑driven articles about plant‑based nutrition, productivity and sustainable food.

FAQ page with common questions about protein amount, vegan status, preparation, and shipping.

Contact page:
Contact form with name, email, message and newsletter signup.

Backend requirements:
Admin dashboard with authentication.
Editable CMS collections for products, blog posts, testimonials, FAQs, hero text, bundles, and nutrition information.
Store contact submissions in database and show them in admin.

Technical goals:
Responsive mobile‑first design, SEO friendly pages, reusable components, clean footer with newsletter signup, social icons and legal links. Ensure the website feels launch‑ready but all content is editable from the admin panel.
